8. CART / ORDER DISTINCTION AND DUPLICATE-CALL NOTES
===================================================
- Order history and the current cart are separate states.
- Existing orders can coexist with an empty cart.
- "No Product in Cart" describes the cart, not the customer's order history.
- Both reported cart-count calls and their responses are retained above.
- The cause of the repeated calls is not established by these responses.
- To investigate: clear Network, disable Keep log, navigate once, compare full
  URLs and methods, and inspect the Initiator for each request.
- Identical final user-ID segments in Network names do not establish that full
  URLs are identical. OPTIONS followed by GET is not two GET requests.
